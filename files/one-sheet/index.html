THE HERO C.O.D.E.™Leadership Programs for Youth

The Hero C.O.D.E. helps young people see themselves as the heroes of their own lives. It borrows the hero's journey and uses it to carry four leadership principles: Courage, Own a new story, Dream big, and Execute boldly.

Three Ways to Bring It

Workshop, Grades 5 to 8

45 to 90 minutes

Middle school and younger. Printed handout.

Workshop, Grades 9 to Graduate

45 to 90 minutes

High school, undergraduate, graduate, young adults. Phones and QR code.

Keynote or Commencement

Keynote 15 to 30 minutes; commencement about 10

Any age, any room size. No materials.

All three formats are available in person or virtually, in English, Spanish, or bilingual.
